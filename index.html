02 Data and Purposes

Depending on the services enabled in a particular game, data may include device and app information, IP address, advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostic events, Google Play Games player identifiers, game progress, achievements and leaderboard scores. We use this information to operate games, enable optional advertising rewards, maintain saved progress and leaderboards, improve reliability, and prevent abuse.

03 Advertising: Google AdMob

Our games may display ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interaction data and technical information for ad delivery, measurement, security and, where permitted, personalization. Where required by law, we request consent using an appropriate consent management mechanism. Users can review applicable privacy choices in the app.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob policies.

04 Google Play Games Services

Where enabled, Google Play Games Services may be used for sign-in, cloud save functionality, achievements and leaderboards. This may involve Google Play Games player identifiers, display names, scores, achievements and game progress. Leaderboard scores and player display names may be visible to other players depending on the leaderboard and account settings. Google's own privacy policy also applies. Cloud save is available only if implemented and enabled in the game.

05 Firebase (Planned)

We plan to integrate Google Firebase in a future update. Firebase is not currently active. If activated, the specific Firebase products used (for example, Analytics or Crashlytics), categories of data processed, purposes, legal bases and retention information will be described here before activation. No Firebase data collection is claimed to occur solely because it is mentioned in this policy.

06 Local Storage

Some game settings or progress may also be stored locally on your device. Local information may be removed when you clear app data or uninstall the game, subject to your device's backup settings. Cloud-stored data may persist separately.

07 Legal Bases and Consent

Where the GDPR applies, processing may rely on consent (for example, for certain advertising or analytics activities), performance of a contract (for necessary game features), or legitimate interests (such as security and fraud prevention), as applicable to the specific processing. You may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Where required, privacy choices can be revisited within the app.

08 Sharing, International Transfers and Retention

Information may be processed by Google and its service providers for the functions described above. Such processing may involve transfers outside your country, subject to applicable legal safeguards. Retention depends on the specific service, account settings and purpose. Google controls retention for data it processes independently; please consult Google's published policies. We retain data under our control only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or legal obligations.

09 Children's Privacy

Our games are not specifically directed to children under 13. If a game is made available to children or other age groups subject to special protections, we will apply the relevant Google Play, advertising and legal requirements, including age-appropriate ads and consent rules where applicable.

10 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive your personal data, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent. You may also complain to a competent data protection authority. Contact nexqori.games@gmail.com for requests concerning data under our control. For information controlled by Google, Google's privacy tools may also apply.

11 Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our control. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

12 Updates and Contact

We may update this policy as our games or third-party services change. The current version and its update date will be published on this page. For privacy questions, contact nexqori.games@gmail.com.
